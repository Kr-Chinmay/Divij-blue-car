Privacy Policy

Mini Driver, Mega Values  ·  Last updated 15 August 2026

Mini Driver, Mega Values is a driving game for young children. This policy explains what the app does and does not do with information, and it is written to be read by a parent rather than by a lawyer.

In short: the game does not collect anything about you or your child. It has no sign-in, no accounts, and asks for no personal information at any point. It does show adverts, and those are provided by Google, which is described below.

What the game collects

Nothing. The game asks for no name, no email address, no age, no location, no contacts and no photographs. There is no account to create and nothing to sign in to.

The name typed on the high score screen is optional, is never sent anywhere, and is stored only on the device itself. It can be anything at all — leaving it blank simply records the score as “Player”.

What is stored on the device

Best scores and the name saved beside them are kept on the phone or tablet where the game is played. They are not uploaded, not shared, and cannot be seen by anyone else.

To erase them, clear the app’s data in the device settings, or uninstall the app. There is nothing to delete anywhere else, because nothing is stored anywhere else.

Adverts

The game shows a single advert banner on its home screen. There are no adverts while the game is being played, and none appear full screen.

Adverts are supplied by Google AdMob. To show them, Google may receive technical information such as the device type, its general region, and the fact that an advert was displayed. This is handled by Google under Google’s own privacy policy, independently of this game.

Because this app is made for children, it is configured so that:

  • adverts are never personalised — they are not based on browsing history, interests or any profile of the user;
  • the app is declared to Google as directed at children, so no advertising identifier is collected for advert requests;
  • advert content is limited to a general audiences rating, so material intended for adults cannot appear.

Google Play

The app is distributed through Google Play. Google may collect information about installations, updates and crashes as part of running the store. That happens for every app on Google Play and is separate from anything this game does.

Internet access

The app asks for internet permission for one reason: to fetch the advert banner. Everything else — the game, its sounds, its artwork — is contained within the app and works with no connection at all.

Children’s privacy

This app is intended for children and is designed accordingly. It collects no personal information from anyone, of any age, and therefore collects none from children. No personal information about a child is ever gathered, stored on our servers, or sold or shared with anyone. We operate no servers.

If you believe the app has somehow collected information about your child, please contact us using the details below and we will respond promptly.

Changes to this policy

If the app changes in a way that affects this policy — for example if the style of advertising changed — this page will be updated and the date at the top revised.
