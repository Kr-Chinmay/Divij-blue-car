Three music options

Tap one to hear it. The engine and the collect sounds are layered in underneath, so you are judging the mix rather than the tune on its own. Best with the phone off silent.

1. Sunny tune

A written melody over a bass note. The most obviously a tune — and the one most likely to wear thin, because a loop is a loop.

2. Driving groove

Shaker, soft kick and a bouncy bass. The tempo follows the speed slider below, so third gear really would sound faster.

3. Endless sparkle

A slow chord pad with bell notes falling at random over it. Never repeats, so it cannot wear out — but it is less obviously a tune.
